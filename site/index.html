How it works

From a spreadsheet to answers in one sitting

No analyst, no formulas, no SQL. Bring the data you already have and Stardeck does the rest.

Build

A whole dashboard in about a minute

Upload an Excel or CSV file, or connect PostgreSQL or MySQL. The AI plans the dashboard an analyst would: the totals, the trend, the breakdowns, the top ten.

  • Every chart’s query is run against your data before it’s added
  • It only reads your database, never changes it
  • It asks about columns that could be misread

Ask

Answers without waiting for an analyst

Ask in plain English and get the answer from your actual numbers, with the query behind it one click away.

  • Follows your dashboard’s dates and filters
  • Asks for a chart? It’s proposed first, added only when you approve
  • Amounts read the Indian way: ₹1.54 Cr, ₹63.35 L

Explore

Click any chart to filter the rest

Click Mumbai and every chart shows Mumbai. Pick a period and every comparison follows. There’s nothing to set up.

  • Date presets, saved filters and click-to-filter
  • Every figure compared with the same period last year

Made for India

Money in lakh and crore, financial years from April to March, and quarters where Q1 is April to June.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where does my data go?

Files you upload are stored on our server in India. Databases stay where they are: Stardeck connects with the access you give it, and only reads.

Where does the AI run?

On our own computer in India, connected to our server over an encrypted private network. Sometimes it pauses, for maintenance for example; your dashboards, filters and links keep working when it does.

Is the AI always right?

No AI is. Stardeck runs every chart’s query before showing it, shows the numbers behind its answers, and never changes a dashboard without your approval. Check figures before big decisions, as you would with a new analyst.

Which data can I use?

Excel and CSV files, and PostgreSQL and MySQL databases that can be reached over the internet.
