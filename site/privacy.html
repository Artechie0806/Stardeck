Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short:

  • We collect only what we need to run Stardeck for you.
  • Your data stays in India, apart from the few exceptions listed below.
  • The AI runs on our own computer in India.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Stardeck is run by {{env "OPERATOR_NAME"}} (“we”, “us”). For your personal data, we are the Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to us at {{env "CONTACT_EMAIL"}} about anything in this policy.

What we collect

  • When you join the waitlist: your email address, plus your name, company, role, where your data lives and what you’d like to see, if you choose to tell us.
  • Your account: your email address and name, and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself). We also keep your role, and when you last signed in. For each signed-in browser we keep when it was last used and the browser’s description (its “user agent”).
  • What you bring to Stardeck:
    • files you upload;
    • the connection details of databases you connect (passwords are stored encrypted);
    • the dashboards, definitions and filters you create;
    • the questions you ask the assistant.
    This data may include personal data about other people, such as your customers. That data is yours: we process it only to run Stardeck for you, as you direct.
  • Technical records:
    • your internet address is held briefly in memory to limit repeated sign-in attempts and sign-ups;
    • we don’t keep logs of who visited which page; our server keeps error logs, without visitors’ addresses, to fix problems;
    • we count how much each workspace uses the AI each day, to apply plan limits.

What we use it for

  • Running Stardeck: sign-in, your dashboards, the assistant, and sharing.
  • Invitations: inviting you from the waitlist, and emailing you about your account.
  • Security: keeping Stardeck secure, and preventing abuse.
  • Improvement: improving Stardeck, using counts and aggregates rather than the contents of your data.

We use your waitlist details because you gave them to us for an invitation (your consent), and your account details because you need them to use Stardeck. We don’t sell personal data, share it for advertising, or use your data to train AI models.

The AI

To build dashboards and answer questions, Stardeck sends the AI:

  • your tables’ and columns’ names, with a few example values;
  • your definitions and your question;
  • for the assistant’s answers, the results of the queries it runs.

The AI model runs on our own computer in India, connected to our server over an encrypted private network. No outside AI company receives your data. The model doesn’t keep what it’s sent, and nothing is used for training.

Who else is involved

  • Our hosting provider runs the server Stardeck lives on, in a data centre in India.
  • OpenStreetMap (maps):
    • Your browser loads map images from OpenStreetMap’s servers, which see your internet address and the area shown.
    • Our server sends the place names in your map charts (such as city names) to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to find their coordinates.
    • These services are outside India. Only place names are sent, never the rest of your data.
  • Backups are encrypted on our server before they’re copied to cloud storage, which may be outside India. They can only be read with a key we keep offline.
  • Email: invitations and account emails go through our email provider.
  • Notifications: when someone joins the waitlist, we may get a notification on our phone with their name, company and email, through a notification service.

We may share data when the law requires it, for example with a court order, and only as much as is required.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist details: until you’re invited or ask to be removed, and at most 12 months after the preview ends.
  • Account and workspace data: while your account or workspace exists. When you ask us to delete it, or your workspace closes, we delete it within 30 days. Copies in backups are gone within a further 30 days.
  • Error logs: until they’re rotated out, as new ones are written.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • see a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete, update or erase it;
  • stop using it, if you gave us consent (for example, to take you off the waitlist);
  • let someone you nominate exercise these rights for you if you can’t.

Write to {{env "CONTACT_EMAIL"}}. We reply within 7 days and resolve requests and complaints within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

The Stardeck app uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in. This website and shared dashboards use no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

Security

Our protections include:

  • encrypted connections everywhere;
  • hashed passwords and encrypted database passwords;
  • each workspace kept strictly apart from the others;
  • read-only access to your databases;
  • limits on repeated attempts;
  • encrypted backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we’ll tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

Children

Stardeck is for businesses and isn’t meant for anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect children’s personal data.

Changes

We’ll post changes on this page and update the date above. We’ll email you about important changes before they apply.
