# Code of conduct

Stardeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project
lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Artechie0806). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove
comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code.
